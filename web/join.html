Run one command and your laptop joins their crew. They can then send it work, and you show up on their leaderboard.

What happens when you run it

  • Two small files are saved to a folder called manifold in your home folder.
  • Your laptop joins the crew and prints a private link. Open it to see your card.
  • Your friend can use up to half your CPU cores while the window is open.

What is shared, and what isn't

  • Shared: your CPU model, core count, memory, and graphics card name and memory.
  • Not shared: your files, your screen or anything else on your computer.
  • Only built-in tasks run. Nobody can run their own commands on your laptop.
  • You stay in control. Close the window or press Ctrl+C to stop sharing at once.
